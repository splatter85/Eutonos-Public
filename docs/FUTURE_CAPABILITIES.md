# Future Capabilities

This document holds desired end-to-end package outcomes that are not current claims or ordered work. Concrete proposed mechanisms belong in `FUTURE_FEATURES.md`; admitted sequencing belongs in `CURRENT_TASK.md`.

## Approved Distribution And Guided Installation

After owner review, a user could obtain a published, authenticated workflow package through a documented channel, inspect its provenance, run a guided installation or upgrade, verify the target, and recover safely from a stopped migration.

Promotion requires an approved distribution authority, package/provenance evidence, end-to-end install/upgrade/recovery proof, and an explicit boundary between release approval and target acceptance.

## Project-Shaped Workflow Variants

Projects in additional domains could initialize an appropriate workflow variant whose owners, checks, overlays, and discovery prompts match the real platform without weakening the universal core or fabricating product facts.

Promotion requires at least one real project per variant, project-native health commands, preservation and uninstall/retirement proof, and evidence that optional guidance does not become universal boot context.

## Independent Comprehension And Recovery Evaluation

A fresh human or model session could independently orient from installed files, identify current truth and next action, execute a bounded recovery scenario, and report limitations without relying on assembly-session context.

Promotion requires an evaluation method that adds evidence beyond deterministic fixture assertions and preserves privacy, repeatability, and a clear release-decision boundary.

## Deterministic Cross-Environment Workflow Automation

A separately authorized Controller could select and dispatch one bounded Action across development environments, verify its returned revision and evidence, recover deterministically, and advance or stop without creating a competing project-truth system.

Promotion requires successful interruption, expired-writer, source-changed-after-test, conflicting-return, and multi-environment round-trip evidence with explicit permissions and human stop boundaries. This capability is shelved.

These outcomes remain unordered and unapproved until promoted into `CURRENT_TASK.md`.
